# Islamic Trading Guide - Avoiding Swap/Interest (Haram)

## Understanding Swap Charges

**Swap** (also called "rollover" or "overnight interest") is charged when you hold a position overnight. In Islamic finance, this is considered **riba (usury/interest)** and is **haram (forbidden)**.

**When Swap is Charged:**
- Most brokers charge swap at **00:00 GMT** (midnight GMT)
- Some brokers use **22:00 GMT** or **23:00 GMT**
- Swap is charged **daily** for each day you hold a position
- **Wednesday** typically has **triple swap** (covers weekend)

## ✅ Strategy 2: Day Trading (Close Before Swap Time)

**Close all positions before swap is charged** (usually before 00:00 GMT).

### Trading Hours to Avoid Swap:
- **Open positions:** During trading hours (8 AM - 10 PM GMT)
- **Close positions:** Before **22:00 GMT** (2 hours before swap time for safety)
- **Never hold overnight:** Close all positions before midnight GMT

### Recommended Trading Sessions:
1. **London Session:** 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM GMT (Best liquidity)
2. **New York Session:** 1:00 PM - 10:00 PM GMT (Close before swap)
3. **London-New York Overlap:** 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM GMT (Best time)

### Avoid:
- ❌ Opening positions after 8:00 PM GMT (too close to swap time)
- ❌ Holding positions overnight
- ❌ Trading on Wednesday (triple swap day)

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Wednesday Triple Swap:
- **Wednesday** has **3x swap** (covers Saturday/Sunday)
- **Avoid opening positions on Wednesday** if you can't close before swap
- **Close all positions before Wednesday 22:00 GMT**

### Weekend Gap Risk:
- Markets close **Friday 22:00 GMT**
- Reopen **Sunday 22:00 GMT**
- **Close all positions before Friday 20:00 GMT** to avoid:
  - Weekend gap risk
  - Potential swap charges

### Broker-Specific Swap Times:
- Check your broker's swap time (usually 00:00 GMT)
- Some brokers: 22:00 GMT, 23:00 GMT
- **Always close 2 hours before swap time** for safety

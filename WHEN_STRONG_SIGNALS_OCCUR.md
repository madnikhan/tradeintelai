## 📈 **Market Conditions for Strong Signals**

### **1. Technical Alignment (50% weight)**

**Strong signals occur when:**
- **RSI extremes:** < 30 (oversold) or > 70 (overbought)
- **MACD crossover:** Clear bullish/bearish crossover
- **Bollinger Bands:** Price at extremes (top or bottom)
- **EMA alignment:** Price above/below EMA20 and EMA50 (trend confirmed)
- **ADX > 25:** Strong trend (trending market)
- **Multiple indicators agree:** 3+ indicators pointing same direction

**Example:**
- RSI < 30 (oversold) = Strong BUY signal
- Price at lower Bollinger Band = Potential bounce
- MACD bullish crossover = Momentum building
- **Result:** Technical score 75-85

---

### **2. Fundamental Clarity (20% weight)**

**Strong signals occur when:**
- **Clear economic data:** Strong GDP, employment, inflation data
- **Interest rate differentials:** Clear carry trade opportunities
- **Central bank policy:** Clear direction (hawkish/dovish)
- **Economic calendar:** Major events with clear outcomes

**Example for GBP/JPY:**
- UK GDP growth strong = GBP strength
- BOJ keeping rates low = JPY weakness
- **Result:** Fundamental score 70-80

---

### **3. Sentiment Alignment (10% weight)**

**Strong signals occur when:**
- **News sentiment:** Clear bullish/bearish news flow
- **Market sentiment:** Risk-on or risk-off clearly defined
- **Multiple news sources agree:** Consistent messaging

**Example:**
- 70%+ bullish news = Strong BUY sentiment
- **Result:** Sentiment score 70-80

---

### **4. COT Data Extremes (10% weight)**

**Strong signals occur when:**
- **Large speculators:** At extreme positions (80th+ percentile)
- **Commercials:** At opposite extreme (contrarian signal)
- **Clear divergence:** Specs vs Commercials disagree strongly

**Example:**
- Large specs 85th percentile long = Strong BUY signal
- **Result:** COT confidence 70-90%

---

### **5. Market Regime Clarity (10% weight)**

**Strong signals occur when:**
- **Clear trend:** Strong uptrend or downtrend
- **Clear range:** Well-defined support/resistance
- **Volatility normal:** Not too high, not too low

**Example:**
- Strong uptrend (ADX > 25) = Trend following works
- **Result:** Regime confidence 70-85%
